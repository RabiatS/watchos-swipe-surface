# Shakshuka for two

Eggs poached in a spiced tomato sauce. About thirty minutes, one pan. The phone can stay propped against the tiles; flick down from the wrist when you need the next step.

# Ingredients

Two tablespoons of olive oil. One onion, sliced thin. One red pepper, sliced. Three cloves of garlic, crushed. One teaspoon of ground cumin. One teaspoon of smoked paprika. Half a teaspoon of chilli flakes, or to taste.

One 400 g tin of chopped tomatoes. Salt and black pepper. Four eggs. A handful of coriander or parsley. Feta to crumble over, if you have it. Bread for the table.

# Method

Warm the oil in a wide pan over a medium heat. Add the onion and pepper with a pinch of salt and cook for about eight minutes, until soft and starting to colour at the edges.

Add the garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli flakes. Stir for one minute until the spices smell toasted, not burnt.

Pour in the tomatoes. Rinse the tin with a splash of water and add that too. Season, then simmer for ten minutes, until the sauce has thickened enough that a spoon dragged through it leaves a trail.

Make four hollows in the sauce with the back of a spoon. Crack an egg into each. Cover the pan and cook for five to seven minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks still wobble.

# To serve

Scatter over the herbs and crumble the feta on top. Bring the pan to the table with bread for dipping. Eat straight from the pan.

# Notes

The sauce can be made a day ahead and reheated before the eggs go in. Harissa stirred into the tomatoes is good. So is a spoon of yoghurt at the end.
